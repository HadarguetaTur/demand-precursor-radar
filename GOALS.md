# Goals, scope, non-goals

**Research question:** Can an event-detection system identify demand-inducing changes using only information observable at announcement time?

**Stage 1 deliverable:** An auditable dataset of historical *change events*, with a pilot of 20 independently verified entries followed (if quality permits) by a frozen sample of 150. Importantly, these are NOT 150 proven demand events.

**Stage 1 must demonstrate:** defensible sampling frame; documented source coverage; immutable-in-intention source snapshots; temporal evidence; exact quote verification; explicit human review; deterministic sampling; failure/rejection logs; repeatable quality report.

**Not yet in scope:** demand labels, product ideas, revenue inference, ML forecasting, real-time opportunity engine, startup SaaS, task automations, legal advice, scraping sites outside explicit terms.

**Success requirement:** Independent reviewer can regenerate sample IDs and document hashes, inspect original source quotations, and reproduce all report counts from checked-in code and a preserved raw-data snapshot.

**Warning:** One-source pilot cannot support claims about all markets/industries. 150 events alone cannot establish predictive ability or profitability.
